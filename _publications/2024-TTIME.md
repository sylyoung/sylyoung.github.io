excerpt: 'This paper accommodates one of the most challenging online transfer learning scenario, where unlabeled EEG samples from the new user arrive in a stream, and immediate classification is performed. When an unlabeled test EEG trial arrives, T-TIME first predicts its labels using ensemble learning, and then updates each classifier by conditional entropy minimization and adaptive marginal distribution regularization. This work enables test time adaptation for calibration-free EEG-based BCIs, making plug-and-play BCIs possible.'
